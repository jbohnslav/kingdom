# Retain queued Council deliveries when targets are unavailable

Follow-up from PR #56 review: https://github.com/jbohnslav/kingdom/pull/56#discussion_r3863659078

When a durable queued Council delivery is resumed after one of its configured targets was removed or renamed, dispatch currently skips the unresolved target and then deletes the delivery. Preserve the pending delivery and provide actionable recovery instead of silently losing it.

This is acknowledged follow-up work and does not block PR #56.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Unresolved unfinished targets leave the durable delivery pending instead of silently deleting it
- [ ] Council chat gives actionable recovery guidance for removed or renamed targets
