### Ticket command classification

| Surface | Class | Evidence and decision |
|---|---|---|
| `create` | keep | Primary ticket-first capture path; supports backlog work and epic parents. |
| `list` | keep | Primary queue/status/dependency/parent inspection with human and JSON output. |

| `current` | keep | Resolves per-execution ticket ownership used by hooks and concurrent sessions. |
| `close` | keep | Canonical typed terminal transition with reason, reference, and lifecycle evidence. |
| `reopen` | keep | Reverses closure without erasing append-only lifecycle history. |
| `status` | keep | Intentional escape hatch for nonterminal custom states; it clears stale context bindings. Canonical start/close/reopen remain preferred. |
| `delete` | keep | Distinct destructive recovery for truly unwanted files, guarded by confirmation and active-peasant checks; it is not a synonym for typed closure. |
| `link` | keep | Symmetric non-ordering relationship, distinct from dependency and parent edges. |
| `unlink` | keep | Required inverse of `link`, with idempotent behavior. |
| `assign` | keep | Explicit manual ownership independent of starting/binding an execution context. |
| `unassign` | keep | Required manual recovery inverse for stale or planned ownership. |
| `parent` | keep | Direct epic/hierarchy organization and the basis of `list --parent`. |

| `deps` | keep | Namespace for dependency mutation and graph inspection rather than four more root ticket concepts. |

All dependency operations remain useful and distinct: `deps add` and `remove`
mutate ordering edges, `tree` explains blockers (including JSON), and `cycle`
validates the global open graph. Classify all four as **keep**. No top-level or
ticket route merits immediate **remove**: the two redundant routes are already
merged aliases, the two obsolete ticket routes have explicit staged removals,
and optional/internal groups can be hidden without breaking existing automation.
