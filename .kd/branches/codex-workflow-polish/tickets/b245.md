# Freeze completed peasant elapsed time at terminal timestamp

`kd peasant status --all` calculates elapsed time against the current clock even
for a `done` peasant. During comparison ticket `648d`, peasant `92ca` had finished
and last activity was 26 minutes earlier, but its displayed elapsed time had grown
to 45 minutes. Persist or derive a terminal timestamp and freeze elapsed duration
once a peasant reaches `done`, `failed`, or another terminal state.

- [x] Terminal peasant rows stop accumulating elapsed time.
- [x] Active peasant elapsed behavior is unchanged.
- [x] Tests cover done, failed, and active sessions with a controlled clock.
